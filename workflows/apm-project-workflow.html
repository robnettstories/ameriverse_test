Always Remember
  • Project number / project name always included in the subject line of emails.
  • At minimum, copy the PM & Project Executive on all project correspondence. Always okay to also copy the Superintendent, Sean, Senior Management, etc. from AmeriTech so they’re tracking.
  • It’s okay to tell the sub or client you don’t have an answer for them in the moment and will get back to them once you discuss with the PM.
  • Always know the CCD.
  • Always know the COR & CO / CS contact info.
